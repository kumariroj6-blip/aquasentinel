EE & Embedded Engineering Guide

1. Bill of Materials (BOM) & Sensor Selection

  • Microcontroller: ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin, dual-core 240MHz, built-in WiFi/BLE).
  • pH Probe: DFRobot Gravity Analog pH Sensor (SEN0161) with BNC connector probe.
  • TDS Meter: DFRobot Gravity Analog TDS Sensor (0-1000 ppm, 3.3V/5V compatible).
  • Turbidity Sensor: DFRobot Gravity TS-300B Optical Turbidity Sensor (0-3000 NTU).
  • Temperature: DS18B20 waterproof digital probe (Dallas 1-Wire protocol with 4.7kΩ pull-up).
  • Actuators: 5-Channel 5V Optocoupled Relay Module controlling 12V DC Normally-Closed Solenoid Valves.
  • ADC Note: For higher 16-bit analog resolution, an external I2C ADS1115 ADC module can replace internal ESP32 ADC1 to eliminate non-linear ADC attenuation.

2. Circuit & Power Architecture

  • Power Isolation: Dual power supply rail — 5V/2A regulated logic supply for ESP32/sensors, and 12V/3A high-current supply for solenoid valves.
  • Flyback Protection: 1N4007 flyback diodes wired across each 12V solenoid coil to suppress inductive voltage spikes during relay cutoff.
  • Relay Optocoupler Isolation: JD-VCC jumper separated on relay board to prevent relay coil transients from resetting the ESP32.
  • Calibration Procedure: Standard 2-point buffer calibration for pH (pH 4.01 and 7.00 buffers) stored in ESP32 Non-Volatile Storage (NVS Flash).
